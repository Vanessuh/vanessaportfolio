Sunwave Escapes

Sunwave Escapes is a vacation booking platform designed to make discovering and planning getaways simple, intuitive, and enjoyable. The project focuses on creating a seamless user experience where travelers can explore destinations, compare options, and book their next escape with ease.

Designing Sunwave Escapes came with the challenge of balancing a visually engaging travel experience with a simple, intuitive booking process.

I focused on reducing information overload by creating clear navigation, organized destination cards, and a straightforward booking flow.

challenge was maintaining a consistent visual style across different screens, which I addressed by developing a cohesive color palette, typography system, and reusable components in Figma. Through iteration and prototyping, I refined the experience to make exploring and booking a vacation feel seamless and enjoyable.

The final prototype combines functionality with a strong visual identity, creating an experience that feels both relaxing and trustworthy while keeping the focus on discovering and booking the perfect getaway.
